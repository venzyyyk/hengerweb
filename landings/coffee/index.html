How we roast

Small batches, roasted to the bean

Every origin gets its own roast profile, dialed on a sample roaster before it ever hits the drum. We roast Monday through Thursday and ship the same week.

No dark-for-the-sake-of-dark. We roast each coffee to the point its best flavors show up — and print the roast date on every bag.
